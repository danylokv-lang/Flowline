# Flowline — Product Overview

## What Is Flowline?

Flowline is an AI-powered daily planner for **Mac and iPhone**. It takes your raw list of tasks and automatically builds a realistic, beautiful schedule for your day — then actively protects your focus by blocking distracting apps while you work.

Think of it as the gap between "I know what I need to do today" and "I actually did it." Flowline fills that gap.

---

## The Problem It Solves

Most people already know they should plan their day. They just don't, because:

- **Planning is slow and boring.** Manually dragging tasks into a calendar takes 20+ minutes. Nobody does it consistently.
- **Plans fall apart instantly.** Even when you make a plan, Instagram and TikTok are one tap away. Focus breaks within minutes.
- **Existing tools are either ugly or complex.** Calendar apps feel corporate. To-do apps don't enforce structure. Productivity apps require hours of configuration.

---

## The Solution

Flowline is built around three core loops:

### 1. Brain-dump → AI Schedule
You type a loose list of everything you need to do today — no friction, no formatting required:
> "math homework, German essay, gym, walk dog, clean room, side project"

You hit **"Plan my day"** and Flowline's AI turns that list into a realistic time-blocked schedule, respecting your wake/sleep time, school or work hours, and natural break patterns. No manual dragging. Done in seconds.

### 2. Focus Blocks → Distraction Blocking
Every task block on your timeline can be "protected." When a protected block starts:
- **Mac:** Focus mode activates, selected websites and apps are blocked.
- **iPhone:** Screen Time filters kick in, hiding distracting apps.

When the block ends, everything goes back to normal automatically. You never have to think about it.

### 3. Check-ins → Smarter Future Plans
After each block, Flowline asks one quick question:
> "Did you finish what you planned? Yes / Partly / No"

Over time, the AI learns your actual pace and energy patterns. It adjusts future plans to be more realistic — shorter sessions when you're tired, harder tasks when you're sharp.

## Core Features

### Task Inbox
- Fast, free-form task entry (comma-separated or line by line)
- Optional: add deadline, estimated duration, or priority
- No forced structure — just dump it in and move on

### AI Day Planning
- One-tap planning: AI converts your task list into a visual time-blocked schedule
- Respects constraints: wake/sleep times, fixed commitments, preferred break lengths
- **Replan button:** if you skip a block or add a task mid-day, AI reshuffles the rest of your schedule around reality

### Visual Timeline
- Clean vertical day view — your day at a glance
- Soft color coding by category: Study, Work, Health, Personal
- Minimal design with smooth micro-animations (plan creation, block completion, transitions)
- Full light and dark theme support

### Focus & Blocking Integration
- You define your distraction list once (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, etc.)
- Per-block toggle: "Protect this block"
- Automatic activation/deactivation — zero manual effort during the day
- Works across Mac and iPhone in sync (Pro)

### Micro Check-ins & Adaptation
- Short post-block prompt: Yes / Partly / No
- AI uses your answers to:
  - Spot patterns (you always drift on afternoon study blocks)
  - Suggest changes (schedule gym earlier, break up long sessions)
  - Silently improve the next plan without you asking

### Students
High schoolers, apprentices, and university students who need structure for studying, homework, and life — but want something that feels good to use, not like a school tool.

**They want:** fast planning, enforced study time, something that actually looks nice on their phone.

### Knowledge Workers & Remote Workers
People doing deep work from home who need help protecting their focus and building consistent daily rhythms — without spending 30 minutes configuring productivity systems.

**They want:** calm, beautiful, automatic. Set it and forget it.

## Design Philosophy

Flowline is built on three design principles:

1. **Speed over completeness.** Getting a plan in 10 seconds beats a perfect plan in 10 minutes.
2. **System does the work.** The app enforces what you decided. You shouldn't have to fight yourself all day.
3. **Calm aesthetics.** Soft colors, generous whitespace, smooth animations. It should feel like relief to open it, not anxiety.
